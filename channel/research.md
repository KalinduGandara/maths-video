# Market research

_Researched 2026-10-15. All figures approximate; third-party numbers are labelled. Re-check monetization rules before applying for YPP._

## Key findings

1. **The YPP bar rises on 1 Feb 2027.** New applicants then need 8,000 watch hours in 365 days, or 20M Shorts views in 90 days. Today it is 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 hours. A channel launching in late October 2026 will not reach 4,000 hours before February, so **plan for 8,000 hours**. Shorts views never count toward watch hours. ([YouTube blog, 2026-08-10](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/), [YPP help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851))
2. **Some features are not available from Sri Lanka.** These are the 500-subscriber fan-funding tier, channel memberships and Super Thanks. Fan income must come from off-platform tools (Patreon, Ko-fi, own products). ([expanded YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240), [memberships](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7636690), [Super Thanks](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10879035))
3. **An AI narrator is allowed if every video is original.** The July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy targets mass-produced, templated output. AI used as a tool is fine when the video shows "creative vision" and "educational value". A non-realistic synthetic narrator over animation does not need the altered-content disclosure. ([policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491))
4. **Long-form earns the money; Shorts are for discovery.**
   - Education and Science long-form RPM: median **$10.22**, middle half $2.31–19.50.
   - Shorts RPM is 3–14% of that (US Shorts about $0.33). Sources: [AIR via overseeros, 2026-08-10](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-rpm-by-niche), [AIR](https://air.io/en/creators-spotlight/still-doubting-shorts-in-2025-were-not-heres-why).
   - Videos of 8 minutes or more get mid-roll ads ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006)).
5. **The "Olympiad Question | Harvard exam" clickbait wave has decayed.**
   - Channels that had 6–8M-view hits now have 3K–18K medians.
   - Daily templated geometry channels (PreMath, Math Booster) are saturated.
   - Copying them is a poor bet.
6. **Famous real competition problems, explained visually, are durable hits.**
   - 3Blue1Brown, Putnam 1992 A6: 16.5M. Windmill IMO 2011 P2: 5.8M. "Last IMO problem AI could not solve": 2.97M in 3 weeks.
   - Numberphile, IMO 1988 Q6: 3.9M. Veritasium, SAT coin: 18–19M.
7. **Open slot: calm, animated, voice-only explanations of olympiad problems for beginners, at 8–15 minutes.** No competitor fills it. The polished channels make 30-minute videos rarely; the olympiad channels use whiteboards and templates. Andy Math (541K) proves that "anyone can follow" puzzles work, with a face and a 5-minute format.
8. **Theorem names have weak standalone search demand** (no pigeonhole video with millions of views was found). Theorem videos should be fed by problem videos ("want the full story?"), not counted on for search.

## 1. Monetization rules (official sources)

**YouTube Partner Program**
- **Standard tier (to 31 Jan 2027):** 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. Shorts-feed hours don't count. ([help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851))
- **From 1 Feb 2027, new applicants:** 8,000 hours in 365 days, or 20M Shorts views in 90 days. Partners already in YPP keep their status. Fan-funding thresholds are unchanged. ([YouTube blog, 2026-08-10](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/))
- **Subscriber requirement after February:** reported to stay at 1,000 ([Shacknews, Aug 2026](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150324/youtube-partner-program-minimum-qualified-public-watch-hours-shorts-views)).
- **Shorts revenue:**
  - Pooled; creators keep 45%.
  - From 1 Feb 2027, a channel needs 10M Shorts views in 90 days to earn Shorts revenue at all. ([help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220))
  - Typical Shorts RPM is $0.03–0.10 ([vidIQ, 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/)).
- **Availability in Sri Lanka:**
  - Standard YPP: available ([country list, fetched 2026-10-15](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7101720)).
  - Expanded (fan-funding) tier, memberships and Super Thanks: not available (sources above).

**Policies**
- **Inauthentic content** (renamed 2025-07-15): bars "mass-produced, generic, repetitive" content and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates". It allows the same intro and outro if "the bulk of your content is different". ([policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392))
- **YouTube's clarification:** Rene Ritchie called the change "minor", aimed at spam ([SEJ, Jul 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/)).
- **Reused content:** bars "readings of other materials you did not originally create" (same policy page).
- **Faceless and TTS channels:** creator reports say they are rejected for "no actual creative voice" (narrated Reddit posts, stock slideshows), not for the format itself ([vidIQ, 2026-03-06](https://stg.vidiq.com/blog/post/types-youtube-channels-dont-get-monetized/)).
- **Disclosure:** only for realistic altered or synthetic content. Animation and AI-written scripts are exempt. ([help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491))

**Tax**
- With a valid tax form (W-8BEN in AdSense), only US-viewer earnings are withheld. Without one, up to 24% of worldwide earnings can be withheld. ([help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10391362))
- A US–Sri Lanka treaty exists ([IRS, updated 2025-11-27](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/sri-lanka-tax-treaty-documents)).
- The royalty rate is about 10%, according to third-party tables only. Confirm with a tax adviser. A treaty claim needs a Sri Lankan TIN.

## 4. Demand

- **Phrasing that recurs:** "can you solve", "olympiad problem", "hardest problem on…", "the question everyone got wrong", "most people get this wrong", "find the area/angle", "[theorem] explained / proof / intuition".
- **Theorem keywords:** no public volume data was found. Run Google Trends comparisons yourself, 5 terms at a time.
- **Viral problem types:** SAT coin rotation, Putnam 1992 A6, Monty Hall, IMO 1988 Q6, PEMDAS disputes, 0.999… = 1, 1+2+3+… = −1/12, semicircle/square geometry, exponent equations.
- **Competition calendar 2026–27:**
  - AMC 10A/12A: **5 Nov 2026**. AMC 10B/12B: **13 Nov**.
  - BMO1: **18 Nov**. Putnam: **5 Dec**. BMO2: **20 Jan 2027**.
  - AMC 8: 21–27 Jan. AIME: about 5–6 Feb (single sitting reported, unconfirmed).
  - USAMO: 20–21 Mar. EGMO: 11–17 Apr. IMO 2027: **Budapest, 16–26 Jul**.
  - Sources: [BMOS calendar](https://bmos.ukmt.org.uk/home/calendar.shtml), [Putnam](https://math.colorado.edu/putnam), [MAA via pw.live](https://www.pw.live/olympiad/exams/american-mathematics-competitions-exam-dates-2026-27), [Cuemath AIME](https://www.cuemath.com/blog/aime-2027-age-rule/).
- **AI at the IMO is a July story every year.** DeepMind and OpenAI both reported gold-level results in 2025 ([TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-deepmind-math-olympiad/)).
- **Candidate problems** (short, elegant, visual): IMO 1959 P1, IMO 1972 P1, IMO 1986 P3, IMO 1995 P2, IMO 2005 P4, IMO 2011 P2, IMO 1988 P6, Putnam 1992 A6, 1957 AHSME P48, 1997 AHSME P26, 2013 AMC 12A P19, 2017 AMC 12A P24, 1982 AHSME P24, Pick's theorem AMC 8 problems. All are listed in `ideas.md`.

## 5. Timing

- **YouTube says publish time doesn't change long-term views,** only how fast the early views arrive ([Rene Ritchie, 2023-11-09](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-on-youtube-november-9-2023-edition/)).
- **Long-form:** studies point to weekday early afternoons and weekend mornings (Sprout Social 2025, CoSchedule Nov 2025). None of them is specific to education.
- **Shorts:** Tuesday at 4 pm and Saturday at 4 pm were best; Monday and Friday were weakest ([Adobe, 2025-07-01](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/best-times-to-post-youtube-shorts)).
- **Education viewing follows the school term:** a summer dip and a September back-to-school dip ([AEA 2023](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2023/program/paper/E3nHNSeb), [Tubefilter, 2025-10-23](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/10/23/youtube-tv-screen-decline-nielsen-the-gauge-back-to-school/)).
- **Cadence:**
  - Regular intervals beat random ones; 0.5–1.5 videos a week suits Education ([AIR, 18K channels, 2026-06-08](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/does-posting-more-on-youtube-help-we-studied-18000-channels-to-find-out)).
  - One study found no effect ([OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/does-consistency-matter-on-youtube)).
- **Shorts should link to the long video** with "Related video" ([Tubefilter, 2023-09-01](https://tubefilter.com/2023/09/01/youtube-related-links-multi-format-creators-shorts-live-long-form/)).
- **Shorts versus long-form views:** one correlational study found long-form views fell after channels adopted Shorts ([arXiv 2402.18208](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18208v2)). Monitor this.
